## Full description

*(Play limit: 4000 characters)*

```
Intelli-Cash replaces the paper register and the cash box ledger for savings
groups, VSLAs and chamas. One phone holds the group's record, and everyone can
see the same numbers.

RUN A MEETING
Record share purchases, the social fund, fines and loan repayments as they
happen, in the order a meeting actually runs. Closing a meeting needs an
official's PIN, so the record cannot be changed quietly afterwards.

LOANS
Issue loans against the group's fund, set your own monthly interest rate, and
see what each member owes with interest included. Repayments update the
balance immediately.

SAVINGS CYCLES AND SHARE-OUT
Track a cycle from the first meeting to the share-out. At the end, the app
works out each member's share, nets off anything they still owe, and shows the
figures before you pay anything out.

WELFARE FUND
Keep the social fund separate from savings. See what is left to share and what
has already been paid out, with every expense tied to the meeting that
approved it.

MEMBERS AND REPORTS
Every member has a statement showing savings, shares, fines and loans. Share
it as text or PDF. Members can sign in on their own phone to see their own
record.

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
Everything is recorded on the phone first. When the network returns, it syncs
to your group's cloud account and nothing is lost. Groups in areas with no
coverage can run entire cycles offline.

COLLECT MONEY YOUR WAY
Connect your group's own M-Pesa (Daraja) or Paystack account so member
payments come straight to the group, or leave collection with the platform
account.

Built in Kenya for Kenyan savings groups.
```
